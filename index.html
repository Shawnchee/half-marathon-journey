Half-marathon plan

Weekly targets · not completed runs

Two priority runs: Easy 1 + the longer run. Easy 2 is optional. Move the days around football; progress only when recovered.

Paces below are provisional guides from the 22 Oct baseline, not mandatory limits. Easy effort (about 3–4/10) and comfortable conversation come first: slower or walk breaks are fine. Race pace is a stretch goal, not a confirmed recommendation.

52 days is a short preparation window. These adjustable targets do not guarantee half-marathon readiness or sub-6:00 pace. Reassess the race after the longer outings; run/walk or postponing remain options.

Recovery, football & weekly adjustments

Leave a rest or easy-recovery day between runs where possible. Treat football as a hard session: skip Easy 2 if tired, and avoid placing the long run next to a hard match. Stop a run that causes pain; do not make up missed distance.

    Easy 1: 6:50–7:30/km. Optional Easy 2 and longer runs: 7:00–7:45/km. These starting guides are not validated training zones; slow down when tired, hot, or on hills. Last year's PBs do not set current training pace.

    Planning context: Great Run beginner plans · NHS running and rest days. This shorter schedule is a custom suggestion, not either published programme.
